# Grown Cookies SEO launch checklist

Cloudflare deployment is intentionally excluded from this checklist. Complete these steps after the approved release has been deployed by the owner.

## Production validation

- Open the homepage, shop, Glasgow collection, gift-box, FAQ, delivery and principal product URLs on `https://growncookies.co.uk`.
- Confirm each URL returns `200`, uses the production canonical, has one H1 and shows the intended title in the browser tab.
- Test the deployed pages with [Google Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results). Product pages should expose `Product` and `Offer`; landing pages should expose their breadcrumbs, lists and visible FAQ data.
- Confirm `/robots.txt` references `https://growncookies.co.uk/sitemap.xml` and that the sitemap contains `/cookies-glasgow` and `/cookie-gift-boxes`.
- Confirm cart, checkout, account, admin and data-deletion pages contain `noindex, nofollow`.

## Google Search Console

1. Open **Sitemaps** and submit `https://growncookies.co.uk/sitemap.xml`.
2. Use **URL inspection** and request indexing for:
   - `https://growncookies.co.uk/`
   - `https://growncookies.co.uk/shop`
   - `https://growncookies.co.uk/cookies-glasgow`
   - `https://growncookies.co.uk/cookie-gift-boxes`
   - the Matcha White Chocolate and Dark Chocolate Maldon Salt product pages
3. Use the three-month Performance view and compare 28-day periods. Do not make decisions from the 24-hour report.
4. Track these query groups separately:
   - Glasgow: `cookies glasgow`, `handmade cookies glasgow`, `cookie collection glasgow`
   - UK delivery: `cookie delivery uk`, `cookies delivered uk`, `fresh cookies delivered`
   - Shopping and gifts: `cookies online uk`, `cookie boxes uk`, `cookie gift box uk`
   - Flavours: `matcha white chocolate cookies`, `dark chocolate sea salt cookies`
5. Record impressions, clicks, click-through rate, average position, landing page and completed orders every 28 days.

## Merchant Center

- Create or verify the Grown Cookies Merchant Center account and website.
- Allow Google to discover products from the website's product structured data, then review price, availability and image warnings.
- Enable free listings where eligible.
- Keep prices and availability on the website authoritative; do not create a separate manual feed that can drift from the shop.

## Glasgow business presence

- Keep Grown Cookies and Akara represented as separate businesses.
- Before applying for a separate Grown Cookies Business Profile at the shared premises, establish permanent fixed Grown Cookies signage and distinct customer-facing business details.
- Prepare evidence showing the collection operation, signage, products, staff access and customer-facing hours for verification.
- Until those conditions are met, seek an accurate contextual link from Akara's website to `/cookies-glasgow`; do not create a misleading storefront listing.

## Reviews, citations and continued growth

- Do not add review stars, ratings or review schema until genuine reviews are visibly published.
- Once the eligible Google profile exists, ask fulfilled customers for honest reviews without incentives or suggested wording.
- Build accurate mentions through real Glasgow markets, event partners, food publications and business directories. Keep the business name, website, contact details and collection wording consistent.
- Add corporate, wedding and event pages only after photographs, lead times, quantities and ordering details are available.
- Review progress every 28 days. Prioritise pages that gain impressions on page two before creating new keyword variants.
